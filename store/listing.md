## Full description (4000 characters max)

Most Arabic words grow out of a root: three letters that carry one idea.
Learn the root ك ت ب, "writing", and book, writer, library and office stop
being four new words and become one family you already know. Arabic Decoded
teaches the language that way.

ROOTS FIRST
• 265 roots: 20 themed campaigns, from the absolute core to arts and
  leisure, plus the Quran's most common roots
• An English memory hook for every root

WORDS THAT GROW FROM THEM
• Nearly 700 words, each with its meaning, transliteration and an
  example sentence
• Every word links back to its root, and every root lists its words
• Short lessons on the patterns Arabic builds words with (the doer, the
  place, the plural…), each arriving just as you have met enough examples

SMART REVIEWS
• Spaced repetition with FSRS, the scheduler modern flashcard apps use:
  each card comes back just before you would forget it
• See when a card will return before you grade it
• A daily goal and a streak to keep you going

READ REAL SENTENCES
• Nearly 14,000 sentences to explore, every word you are studying
  underlined in the colour of how well you know it
• Open any word to see the sentences it appears in

AND MORE
• A Quran mode: the 105 most common roots of the Quran, most common
  first, sharing your progress with the rest of the app
• A complete alphabet course for absolute beginners
• Famous stories of Arab culture, one after each campaign
• Collections and favourites, multi-select, and practice runs
• Learning stats by day, month or year
• Five Arabic typefaces, and a night mode
• Works fully offline. An optional account backs up your progress and
  syncs it between your devices.

No ads. No tracking.
