# Arabic Decoded: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

Arabic Decoded is an app for learning Arabic vocabulary. This page says
what information the app handles and why.

## Using the app without an account

You can use the whole app without signing in. In that case everything you
do (which cards you have studied, your review history, your favorites and
collections, your settings) is stored only on your device and is never
sent anywhere.

## If you create an account

An account is optional. Its only purpose is to back up your progress and
keep it the same across your devices. If you create one, the following is
stored on our backend, which is hosted by Supabase:

- your email address and an encrypted form of your password, used to sign
  you in;
- your learning progress: for each card you have studied, when it is next
  due and how well you know it;
- your review history: the dates on which you reviewed cards;
- your favorites and collections.

This information is used only to provide that sync. It is not sold, not
shared with advertisers, and not used to build a profile of you.

## Things the app does not do

- It shows no adverts and contains no advertising or analytics trackers.
- It does not access your contacts, location, photos, camera or
  microphone.

## Reading aloud

The "Listen" button uses your device's own text-to-speech. Depending on
how your device is set up, the maker of that speech engine (for example
Google or Apple) may process the text being read. The app sends nothing
itself.

## Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from Account > Delete account
inside the app. This permanently removes your account and everything
stored with it from the backend, and clears your progress on that device.
Data from using the app without an account is removed by uninstalling the
app.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not
intended for them.
